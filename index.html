About Mackdonald Dulo

Mackdonald Dulo is a Kenyan scholar from Mombasa, Kenya. He was born on 26 March 2007 and began his primary education at Linet Primary School before continuing his education at Mombasa Primary School. He later joined Kenyatta High School.

Mackdonald has an interest in football and has also earned a Wavumbuzi certificate. His profile includes information about his education, achievements, personal background and interests.
